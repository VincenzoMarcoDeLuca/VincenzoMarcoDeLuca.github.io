About

Research at the intersection of AI, networks and people.

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Information Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Trento. My doctoral research is supported by a scholarship on AI coaching for human-machine teaming.

My work explores how machine learning and graph-based representations can model complex interactions between people, agents and environments, while producing interpretable and actionable insights.

A central application of my research is the modeling of surgical teams, where multimodal observations, temporal interactions and counterfactual reasoning can be combined to better understand teamwork and support human decision-making.

Research

Research interests

01

Graph Neural Networks

Representation learning on structured and relational data, with a focus on temporal, higher-order and interaction graphs.

02

Human–AI Teaming

AI systems designed to support collaboration between humans and intelligent agents through actionable and interpretable recommendations.

03

Surgical Team Modeling

Multimodal and time-expanded graph representations for understanding dynamics, interactions and teamwork in operating rooms.

04

Explainable AI

Methods for improving the interpretability, reliability and actionability of graph-based machine learning models.

05

Computational Social Science

Computational approaches to understanding social interactions, group behavior and relational dynamics.

06

AI for Healthcare

Machine learning methods applied to biomedical and healthcare problems, including drug-target interaction prediction and clinical teamwork.
