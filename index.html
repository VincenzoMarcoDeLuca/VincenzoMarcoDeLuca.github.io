02 / Research

From relational learning to actionable AI.

My current PhD work investigates how machine learning can support human teams without obscuring the reasoning behind model outputs. A central theme is the use of graph-based representations to model interactions, temporal dynamics and counterfactual interventions.

My research topics include Graph Neural Networks, explainability, computational social science and AI for healthcare. In particular, my recent work focuses on surgical team dynamics and on models that can turn multimodal observations into interpretable suggestions.

I am also interested in machine learning for language processing and computer vision, optimization, logic, planning, knowledge engineering and quantum computing.
