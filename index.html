About

Research at the intersection of AI, networks and people.

I am pursuing a PhD in Information Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Trento. My doctoral scholarship concerns AI coaching for human-machine teaming.

My research explores how machine learning and graph-based representations can model complex interactions between people, agents and environments, while producing interpretable and actionable insights.

One application is the modeling of surgical teams, where multimodal observations, temporal interactions and counterfactual reasoning can help understand teamwork and support human decision-making.
